Enroll a device

Create an invitation for a device owner. This invitation does not grant device permissions.

Owner enrollment steps

1. Owner opens invitationInvitation identifies the organization and expires quickly.
2. Owner installs PHANTOM agentApp explains collected data and purpose before enrollment.
3. Owner approves permissionsLocation and other capabilities are individually granted in Android settings/prompts.
4. Device appears in dashboardSecure registration, revocation and audit history are required.
