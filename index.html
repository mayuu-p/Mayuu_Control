  2. Open it on the device/browser owner wants to enroll.

  4. Dashboard receives limited browser data and server-observed IP.
  5. GPS is separate and opt-in.

Device browser enrollment

This is a voluntary enrollment invitation for PHANTOM RECOVER. Nothing is collected until you accept below.

If you continue, the service will receive basic browser/device details (browser/platform, language, timezone, screen size), your public IP as observed by the server, and a timestamp. These details can be used to identify this browser session. No IMEI, SIM data, contacts, messages, camera, microphone, or files are accessed.
By tapping Continue & enroll, you confirm that you have read the disclosure above and agree to this browser session enrollment. GPS, camera, microphone, and screen sharing remain OFF until you separately choose to share them and approve the browser/OS prompt.
